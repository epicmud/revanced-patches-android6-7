# NEARING EOL!
YouTube 20.23.40 is the last version you can spoof, and things become kinda broken.
## It works, I guess. At least you can browse, watch videos
If you really want to try, here's how to do it.
1. Download the manager apk from here https://github.com/epicmud/revanced-patches-android6-7/releases/tag/fix-patches-v5.8.1-dev.2
2. Download YouTube 17.34.36 apk (not the bundle) from apkmirror.
3. Install it, open and follow what it tells you to do.
4. Go to settings, scroll down and click "Alternative sources".
5. Change patches organization to epicmud, click ok, wait about 30 seconds for it to download, and then restart the app.
6. Select Patcher, select an app, then click on storage at the bottom. Select your Youtube apk, wait, then patch and install.
7. Open RVX. Follow the instruction to wait for server side layouts, then click the restart button after 10 seconds.
8. Go to settings > app version spoofing. First, enable Freeze layout updates, then restart. Next go back to settings and enable spoof app version. Click cancel, and change spoof app version target to 19.05.36. Hit restart.
9. Go back to settings, and click Edit spoof app version. Change it to 20.23.40, the last working version. Restart.
## Success! You now have a partially broken, but still workinng YouTube app.
